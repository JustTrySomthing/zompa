## What you can do today

- **Play Cats vs Dogs.** Install it from **Games → Get Games**. Play solo, pass and play on one phone, or take turns online. Choose a friend from **My Games → Play → New Online Match**, or choose a game from the gamepad in a friend chat. Tap **Invite** to send it. Play starts a new round. **Active Games** shows a count and lets you resume or end a local round or online match. Back leaves unfinished games resumable. After an online match, invite the same friend to play again or leave the board. In-game chat previews appear briefly while you play.
- **Connect with friends.** Create a Chatmail profile, share an invite link or QR code, exchange encrypted one-to-one messages, and place voice calls. Message alerts open the matching conversation. Tap website addresses or YouTube previews to open them in the app. Search, pin, and mute chats from the Chats list. Search messages in Feed or browse, filter, preview, and save shared attachments in Media.
- **Manage your game library.** A fresh install starts with an empty library. Add official `.zomp` games and remove them from **My Games**. Open the import button for compatible `.love` files on your device.

[Submit a game or update its catalog artwork](https://github.com/JustTrySomthing/zompado-games#submit-a-game).

Downloaded games require Android 10 or later and a sandboxed WebView renderer. Open **Settings → Game audio** to adjust volume, mute the game, or control whether its volume lowers during a call. The circled speaker mutes all game sound. SDK games also support separate music and effects controls. [Game SDK and toolbar behavior](sdk/README.md).

After Android stops the process, Zompado checks for new activity on Android’s schedule. A call alert lets you answer if the call is still ringing. Reopen the app after using Android’s **Force stop**. **Settings → Setup** and **Help** cover alerts and permissions.

In a chat, tap the phone icon to open the dialer, then tap **Call** to reach the contact shown. During a call, use **End call** to hang up. Calls also support mute, handset and speaker routing, and a camera toggle.
